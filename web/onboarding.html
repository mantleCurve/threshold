The agreement

Here is exactly what will happen, and when.

Nothing on this page is a default someone else chose for you. Read it, change what you want, and it takes effect the moment you save.

Step one

Who sees each rung

Your caregivers see only the rows you switch on — plus tiers 4 and 5, which nobody can switch off.
Tier What puts you here What Threshold does Your caregiver sees it
0 Baseline Nothing is happening You checked in. The day is ordinary. Nothing, unless you ask. Never shared
1 Elevated Something was missed missed check-ins. Threshold asks how you are. That's all. Never shared
2 Craving You said something that matters You tell it you're craving, or it hears it in what you say. Plays a voice you know. Offers to reach one person. Grounding.
3 Active use You are using You say you're using, or you've gone quiet after saying you would. Stays with you. Keeps you talking. Shows you the 911 words and your Good Samaritan protection before you need them.

Step four

Invite someone to support you

Nobody can attach themselves to you. Not a family member, not us. The only way anyone sees any part of this is that you generate a code here and give it to them yourself.

Read this to them, or show them the screen

They enter it at /register/caregiver. It works once, for one person, and stops working after 24 hours. Generate another any time — there is no limit, and an unused code costs nothing.

Step five

The voice Threshold speaks in

You will hear this at 3am. Pick one you can stand.

This is a synthetic voice, and it is the app speaking as the app — it never claims to be a person. Memory Vault clips are different: those are real recordings of real people, played exactly as recorded. Threshold does not and will not imitate anyone's voice.

Saved on this device only.

The promise

What Threshold will never do

  • Never share what you said. Your caregiver sees tiers, never transcripts.

  • Never keep a log you can't see. There is one log, and it's on your event page.

  • Never let a model decide a tier. Every escalation is a written, auditable rule.

  • Never make you argue with it. "I'm okay" always works, at every tier.

Changes take effect immediately.
