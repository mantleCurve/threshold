Exactly what you can and cannot see about your member

You can see

  • The tier, whenever it is one they have shared with you.
  • Tier 4 and tier 5, always — nobody can switch these off, and they know that.
  • The reason the tier changed, in the system's own words.
  • What the system did in response, and when.
  • Their address and entry code, at tier 4 and above only.

You cannot see

  • Anything they said. Transcripts are never shared with you.
  • Which substances were involved.
  • Tier 2 and tier 3, unless they have turned those on for you.
  • Location, at any tier below 4.
  • Anything at all after they stand down — the alert closes with it.

Your member sets these on their own ladder page and sees this same list. There is no second view of them that you are not being shown — and they invited you here, which is the only way you could be.

Your voice, if you want to offer it

You can record yourself reading three short passages, and Threshold will build a synthetic copy of your voice from them. If you then choose to share it, and your member chooses to turn it on, the app will read its prompts to them in a voice that sounds like yours.

What it is not: it is not you. It is an AI recreation, it is labelled as one on screen every single time it speaks, and it will never say you are there, listening, or on your way — because you would not be, and telling someone in crisis otherwise would be a lie told in your voice.

Your Memory Vault recordings are untouched by this. Those are real recordings of you, and they play exactly as you recorded them.
