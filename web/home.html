The escalation ladder

Six states. As danger rises, the interface takes things away.

Below the threshold, Threshold asks. At a medical emergency, it clears the screen around the actions that matter.

  1. 0BaselineNothing is happening
  2. 1ElevatedA check-in was missed
  3. 2CravingYou asked for support
  4. 3Active useThreshold stays with you
  5. Threshold sits here

Before the crisis

Tolerance changes quietly. Threshold says the number out loud.

A hospital stay, detox, incarceration, or time away from use can reduce tolerance quickly. The remembered amount does not change with it.

11 days since tolerance reset Your previous amount may now be fatal.

Consent as structure

A caregiver cannot add themselves to your life.

You generate the invitation. You decide what they see below the emergency threshold. Revoking the link takes effect immediately.

What we deliberately did not build

Trust comes from the things the product refuses to do.

No sobriety score

No streak to protect and no penalty for telling the truth.

No hidden caregiver view

The member can see the same permission boundary the caregiver receives.

No AI safety decision

Language can adapt. The escalation state remains deterministic and auditable.

No continuous location trail

Location is requested only when the emergency interface needs it.

Set it up on a calm day. That is the whole idea.

Member and caregiver accounts use verified email. Phone numbers are not OTP-verified.
